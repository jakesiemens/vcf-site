Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Victory Christian Fellowship ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our church media applications and website (victorychristianfellowship.ca) handle information.

1. Information We Collect

Our sermon publishing portal only accesses authorized YouTube accounts to upload Sunday sermon videos, titles, descriptions, and thumbnails directly to the official church YouTube channel.

2. Use of Google and YouTube APIs

Our application uses Google APIs and YouTube Data API v3 solely for uploading and managing church media content. We do not sell, distribute, or share user data or credentials with third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Authentication credentials (tokens) are stored securely and locally on church administrator equipment to facilitate automated video publishing. No personal data is stored externally.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please contact us at victorychristianfellowshipca@gmail.com or visit us in Williamsburg, NB.
